    Session 07 — The Woman in the Window

    Session: 7 — November 3–4, 1884 Status: Resolved

    Overview

    The party woke to discover they had all shared the same dream — a woman in white descending a staircase toward a young woman who filled them with dread. By the end of the session, that young woman was dead in an alley near the docks, drained of blood. The vampires have arrived in Seattle.

    What Happened

    The Shared Dream

    All three party members had the same dream the night before. An interior balcony. A woman in white descending stairs, back to them. At the bottom: a young woman, mousy brown hair, freckled, serene and reaching upward. Dread — not from the young woman but from the one descending. Father Miller ran down to intervene and was suddenly back at the top beside Jim. Jim called "Stop." Everything went dark. Heavy iron lock clicking.

    The Investigation

    Horace Clayton found them at breakfast — three or more people missing in a six-block radius. Latest: Clara Marsh, seamstress, missing four days. He took it personally.

    • Agnes Keller (landlady): Clara's behaviour changed 3–4 weeks ago. Found wandering at 2am. Red Line Insurance certificate arrived the same time. Jim found a scrap of paper behind the dresser: Thursday 9pm, big flowy handwriting, not Clara's.
    • Mabel Whitmore (dress shop owner): Clara let go Tuesday — didn't argue, just nodded and left like it didn't matter.
    • Ruth Hennigan (coworker): A man with glasses came to the shop a month ago asking for Clara by name, said he was following up on a policy. Clara once left mid-shift and came back confused. Ruth followed her once — Clara was standing on First Avenue staring at a building, unresponsive to her name. Ruth agreed to show them the building after her shift.

    The Body

    Police commotion near the docks. Deputy Lawson waved them in. A woman in an alley — mousy brown hair, wrong kind of pale. Two deep puncture wounds on the neck. Completely exsanguinated — far more than the wounds account for. All three recognised her immediately: the woman from their dream, the one at the bottom of the stairs.

    Mama Duvall stepped back. Vampire train flashback. Strong Dominican accent: "Vampires."

    The Window

    Something made them all look up. A block and a half away, a lit window on the third or fourth floor of a building. A shape watching. Then gone. Jim's survival instinct: heading in the right direction. That building is Red Line Insurance.

    Outcome

    • Clara Marsh confirmed dead — vampire victim
    • Red Line Insurance identified as the connection point
    • Ruth Hennigan will show them the building — this is the Session 8 opening
    • The vampires now know the party is on the case

    GM Only

    • The shape in the window was Petrolena Vulpe. She watched them specifically.
    • The man with glasses who visited Whitmore & Sons was Harvey Purvis — Vulpe's thrall, now chained in the building.
    • Mama Duvall's vampire train trauma was triggered. Vulpe or Harvey may sense something unusual about her.
    • Horace has publicly named Red Line in the Post-Intelligencer. Clock is ticking.
