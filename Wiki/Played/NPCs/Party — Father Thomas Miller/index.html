    The Priest — Father Thomas Miller

    [!tip] Player Character Full Name: Father Thomas Miller Player: Paul Waggott Religion: Catholic Status: Alive Harrowed: No

    "The Priest" is the GM/wiki alias. He is known publicly as "the Reverend Father." His telegraph identity is Father Thomas Miller.

    Player Notes

    Submitted by Paul Waggott via Telegraph Office, 2026-04-26

    Father Thomas Miller is a Catholic Priest, specialising in exorcisms and the eternal war with the forces of hell. He is in his late 40s, 6' tall, with black hair. He originally hails from the Boston area, and studied in Rome. He is a member of The Twilight Legion.

    Character Overview

    A Catholic priest operating in the Pacific Northwest. Devout. Practical about the supernatural in a way that probably unsettles his superiors back east. Has seen enough in Seattle to reframe his theology considerably.

    Physical

    Late 40s. 6 feet tall. Black hair. Originally from Boston. Studied theology in Rome. Carries himself with the quiet authority of a man who has seen worse things than whatever is currently happening and survived them all.

    Submitted by Paul Waggott via Telegraph Office, 2026-04-26.
