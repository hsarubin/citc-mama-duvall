    Deputy Chandra Lawson

    [!info] At a Glance Role: Deputy Sheriff, Seattle Location: Seattle Faction: Law Status: Alive (recurring)

    Description

    The almost always cheery Deputy hails from Kansas with a thick country accent — and an occasional Haitian influence she picked up from her "grams," the same woman who taught her voodoo. Curious to a fault. Asks detailed questions about the party's investigations, how they defeated some creature, what ammo they used. She's genuinely their best friend if they let her be. Carries a Colt Lightning and a club.

    Motivation

    Keep Seattle safe. She's good at this job and she knows it. Doesn't need help — but she'll take it when offered, which puts the party in a complicated position of being useful and unofficial.

    Personality

    Direct. Dry. Loyal to people who prove they deserve it. Will tell you exactly what she thinks and what she can and cannot do, and won't apologize for either.

    Background

    Mother was a slave in Louisiana who learned voodoo from her own mother. Chandra keeps this quiet, but uses it when it helps crack a case. She's pushing for the title of "Assistant Chief of Police" but Woolery hasn't agreed yet.

    She's a member of the Twilight Legion — which the party may or may not know depending on how that introduction unfolds.

    Role in Campaign

    • Party's primary law enforcement contact
    • Bridge between official Seattle and the weird
    • Complained to by women whose husbands visit Red Line Insurance and come home "changed" — a clue toward Bloodsucking Fiends
    • Act Three: Captured by howlers; appears as a simulacrum created by the Bone King at the edge of his domain (legless, cheerfully warning the party to turn back)
    • Recurs throughout all three Acts; named as a potential "victim" in the Bone King's trials
