    Annie Paige

    [!info] At a Glance Role: Archaeologist / Explorer Location: Seattle → Green River Gorge → Bone King's cabin Faction: Independent Status: Alive (recurring)

    Description

    Short brown hair, khaki pants, yellow top, blue scarf. Pith helmet. Carries an archaeology kit and a Colt Rainmaker. Usually slightly drunk when first encountered. Looks younger than she is.

    Background

    Born in Massachusetts. Worked with her father in archaeology from childhood. Educated at Oxford. Royal Geographic Society membership recently revoked — she's furious about it and will tell you why at length, whether you asked or not.

    Has a Necronomicon skull fragment in her bag. Believes she found proof of alien structures at Cahokia. Nobody believes her. She may be right.

    Motivation

    Prove everyone who doubted her wrong. Find the thing that can't exist. She's brilliant and chaotic and impossible to stop once she's locked onto something.

    Personality

    Loud. Funny. Incapable of leaving anything alone. Gets drunk and overshares classified archaeological secrets. Completely fearless in the way that only people who haven't fully processed their own mortality are.

    Stat Block

    Attributes: Agility d6, Smarts d8, Spirit d8, Strength d6, Vigor d6 Skills: Academics d8, Athletics d6, Common Knowledge d6, Fighting d4, Healing d4, Notice d6, Persuasion d4, Science d4, Shooting d6, Stealth d4 Pace: 6 | Parry: 4 | Toughness: 5 Edges: Brave Hindrances: Curious Gear: Pith helmet, archaeology kit, Colt Rainmaker (Range 12/24/48, Damage 2d6, AP 1)

    Key Connections

    • Dr. Mary Ann Connor (Territory University) — knows Annie and considers her "preposterous" theories about Viking habitation outrageous. Annie considers the feeling mutual.
    • Muckleshoot Territory — Annie's Viking site is in or near sacred Muckleshoot land; the tribe has asked for help turning her away (see The Draugr, p.88)

    Appearances in Campaign

    1. Little Annie Orphaned (book p.65) — met in a saloon, drunk, recently expelled from RGS
    2. The Draugr (book p.88) — at the Green River Gorge, insists on going in
    3. The Bone King (book p.122) — at Yashulid's cabin
