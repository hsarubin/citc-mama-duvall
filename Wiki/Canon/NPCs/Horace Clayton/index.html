    Horace Clayton

    [!info] At a Glance Role: Reporter, Seattle Post-Intelligencer Location: Seattle — his beat is the whole city Faction: The Press Status: Alive (recurring)

    Description

    Wiry. Always has a notebook. Never quite seems to be paying attention and always is. Drinks moderately. Remembers everything.

    Background

    Has been covering Seattle since before it was worth covering. Knows where most bodies are buried — figuratively. A few literally, after the last few weeks.

    Works under Editor Thomas Prosch and Owner Leigh Hunt at the Post-Intelligencer.

    Voice

    Dry wit. Victorian propriety with a raised eyebrow underneath it. Prints the official line but includes details that make the reader think. Never lies — just selects. Will describe a supernatural event in language that sounds mundane while including every detail that makes it obviously not.

    "The conductor maintains it was merely a freak bush leaping from the tracks in the dead of night."

    Motivation

    The truth. But more practically, a good story. He's not reckless — he won't print something that gets someone killed. But he's watching everything and writing it all down somewhere.

    Personality

    Professionally warm. Remembers your name, your drink, what you said three weeks ago. Asks questions that sound like small talk. Will absolutely print the thing you said off the record if it's interesting enough and you didn't say "off the record" first.

    Background (from Book)

    Seattle's first African American journalist. Covers politics and occasionally stranger stories. A trusted ally of Tombstone Epitaph reporter Lacy O'Malley.

    Works under Editor Thomas Prosch (taciturn; only interested in facts; at odds with Clayton's instincts but defers to ownership) and Owner Leigh Hunt (bullish on sharing information and revealing truth; frequent thorn in the Agency's side).

    Agent Tatum Black works with Clayton occasionally — helping him find the line between reporting the truth and sending the public into an outright panic.

    The Post-Intelligencer building is on the northeast corner of Yesler Way and Post Street.

    Role in Campaign

    • Primary in-world narrator voice
    • Party's interface with the public perception of their actions
    • Quietly accumulating information the party doesn't know he has
    • Will become more significant if the party cultivates or burns the relationship
