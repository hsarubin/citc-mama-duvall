    H.P. Lovelace

    [!info] NPC — Player Created An in-world author of horror and romance fiction, based in Providence, Rhode Island. Created by Stalker Jack (Mama Duvall's player) as part of Mama Duvall's backstory. Mama Duvall reads his work extensively.

    Character Notes

    A writer of dark, atmospheric fiction. Health uncertain. Signs correspondence simply as "Lovelace, Providence." Replies are brief, formal, and slightly ominous in tone — the pen of a man who lives too close to whatever he writes about.

    Relationships

    Character Notes
    Mama Duvall Fan and correspondent. Lovelace received her message warmly.

    Telegraph Exchange — 2026-04-26

    Mama Duvall sent: Fan message praising his work and wishing him good health; hoping his next novel is not far away.

    Response: MAMA DE VALL SEATTLE STOP YOUR KIND WORDS RECEIVED WITH GREAT PLEASURE STOP THE DARK MUSES ARE RESTLESS AND THE NEXT TALE STIRS IN THE SHADOWS STOP HEALTH UNCERTAIN BUT THE PEN DOES NOT REST STOP PROVIDENCE WATCHES OVER US ALL STOP LOVELACE PROVIDENCE RHODE ISLAND STOP

    GM Notes

    • Pure character development — no plot relevance. Bot can handle freely.
    • If Mama Duvall writes again, Lovelace can respond in this voice: world-weary, literary, slightly gothic.
    • Keep him in Providence — no reason for him to come west.
