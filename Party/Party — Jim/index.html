    Jim

    [!tip] Player Character Archetype: Old Cowboy Status: Alive (recovering) Harrowed: No

    Character Overview

    Old cowboy type. Arrived with Mama Duvall on the October 20th express. Veteran of enough to be unimpressed by most things. Apparently including cardiac episodes.

    Key Character Moments

    • Session 0 — Arrived in Seattle with Mama Duvall on the October 20th express. Something happened on the train. He doesn't remember.
    • Session 1 — Investigated the phantom of Frye's Opera House
    • Sessions 2–3 — Fought the Sack-Headed Man. Had a cardiac episode. Survived. Described the experience as "Tuesday."
    • Sessions 5–6 — Haunted Hotel. Mama Duvall destroyed his hat putting out the boiler fire.
    • Session 7 — Batsquatch on the Yesler-Leery rooftop. Gave an interview to Horace Clayton.

    Active Threads

    • The Heart — Had a significant cardiac episode during the Sack-Headed Man fight. What are the ongoing effects?
    • The Hat — Mama Duvall destroyed it putting out the boiler fire at the Grand Central. Running thread.
